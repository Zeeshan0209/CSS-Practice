Ironman

Iron Man is a Marvel superhero whose real name is Tony Stark (Anthony Edward Stark) a genius inventor, engineer,and billionaire industrialist who creates advanced powered armor suits and uses them to fight threats around the world.He first appeared in Tales of Suspense #39 in 1962 and became one of the founding members of the Avengers.

First movie 2008

Tony Stark is a brilliant weapons manufacturer who is captured by terrorists. While imprisoned, he builds a powered armor suit to escape. After returning home, he decides to stop making weapons and creates a more advanced suit, becoming Iron Man.
